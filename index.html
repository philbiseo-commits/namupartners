Business categories

Practical categories for Korean founders entering Brisbane.

We focus on categories where local setup, cost structure, and launch mistakes are especially important.

Flagship category

Cafe

Strong repeat demand, but profitability changes dramatically depending on site flow, coffee setup, counter configuration, and labour design.

Lean format

Sushi / takeaway

A tighter footprint can work well here, but prep, refrigeration, hygiene flow, and consistency matter from day one.

Korean restaurant

Demand can be strong, but menu complexity, labour pressure, and kitchen design need sharper planning.

Korean BBQ

High concept strength and spend potential, but ventilation, canopy requirements, and capex make it a more selective entry option.

Cleaning business

Lower setup cost than food, but scheduling, staffing reliability, quality control, and repeat customer systems become the core engine.
